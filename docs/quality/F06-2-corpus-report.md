| Reviewer | none |
| Corpus status | **draft** |
| Draft clauses | **36** |
| **Reviewed clauses** | **0** |

The annotations were written by reading the synthetic text, **not** by running the pipeline and copying
its output — the model being evaluated was not used to generate ground truth. But an AI assistant is not
a human reviewer, and this report does not pretend otherwise. The corpus is a **workflow demonstration**:
it proves the schema can carry provenance and review state, and it gives reviewers something concrete to
correct.

## 10. Verdict

**PARTIAL.** The corpus schema, the validation rules, the coverage tooling and a diverse 36-clause draft
exist and are tested. What does not exist is the thing the task is named after: **human annotation**.
Until two people have reviewed these clauses — and any corpus expansion has been adjudicated — the only
true statements are "the corpus covers all 14 categories" and "the annotations are drafts". Anything
about classification quality remains unproven.
